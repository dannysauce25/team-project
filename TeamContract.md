## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---

### Communication

  * Updates on each other part

  * Everyone has one strike if they miss a deadline or do something that pushes back the schedule (Punishment: Buy boba for the entire group) 
    
* Respectful and inclusive behaviour are necessary for smooth and productive communication. What are some respectful and inclusive behaviours you expect when communicating with each other during lectures, labs, or outside of class? ## Decision Making

* How will decisions about your team project be made? (Examples: By majority vote, by unanimous vote, etc.)
  * Majority vote (other than time). For all team meetings we will work based off everyone schedule and preference. 
  
---
## Conflict resolution

* How will your team resolve conflicts? (Example: by listening to each other's side of the issue and attempting to reach a compromise. By consulting a TA or instructor as a team, if a resolution has not yet been reached.)
  * Case by Case Basis.
  * Solve with Communication.
  * If we have a conflict, we will state it immediately while be honestly stating our option being respectful.
  * If we are unable to work with another member. They will hold a one to one call to discuss their emotions. If they are still unable to work with each other, the remainder group will communicate their parts to each other.
